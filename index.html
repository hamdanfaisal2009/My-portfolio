About Me

Who I am, and how I got here

I'm Hamdan, a full-stack developer based in Karachi, Pakistan, building web products that hold up under real usage — not just in a demo. I started out obsessed with the frontend, fell in love with backend systems along the way, and now I'm happiest somewhere in between: designing an API, then building the interface that makes it feel invisible.

Outside of client work, I maintain a couple of open-source utilities, write about practical engineering on my blog, and I'm slowly working through a personal challenge to ship one small tool every month.
